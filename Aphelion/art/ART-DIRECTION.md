# Aphelion art direction

Created after reading the complete Aphelion.panda story and its picture-field references.

## Visual continuity

- Anonymous explorer: scratched ivory EVA suit, charcoal joints, muted teal backpack and panels, dark visor, amber wrist light. The face and identity remain concealed.
- ECHO: represented by the suit's amber light; no separate embodied character.
- Planet: cracked chalk-white mineral ground, violet/coral skies, paired mint and peach moons.
- Relay: corroded skeletal dish tower and damaged struts.
- Forest: dark branching roots, cyan/emerald luminous veins, translucent porous growths.
- Companion: low indigo quadruped, luminous green veins, turquoise eyes, translucent swept crest. Appears in the forest branch and its endings.
- Palette: jewel-toned atmospheric science fiction, shifting into indigo for dusk and warm opal light for Roots.

## Story coverage

Ten images cover the title, crash site, ECHO's awakening, tower, forest, and all five endings. The tower includes three abandoned suits. The Loop uses reflections to suggest repetition. The Signal Answered shows three reply lights. Roots portrays belonging rather than body horror. The Ship Remembers shows the recovered recorder and a path forward.

The common forest image previews several discoveries because the source reuses that image across the forest branch. Illustrations are atmospheric interpretations; the game text remains authoritative.

## Files and integration

All ten runtime JPEGs are 1983 x 793, approximately 5:2, matching the existing isotropic picture field. They are saved under the exact filenames already referenced by Aphelion.panda at S:\Aphelion\art\. No source or compiled-program change is required.

gallery.html presents the complete set. prompts.json records the full prompts and original output locations. Images were made with the built-in image-generation tool, using the title image as a shared visual reference. The tower received a correction to remove a companion that does not belong in that branch. Original PNGs remain at the recorded generation locations; runtime JPEGs use quality 92 without resizing or cropping.

Validation: all ten JPEGs were decoded and matched to source references. Artwork was visually reviewed during generation. The live BR picture field has not been exercised for this asset-only change.
